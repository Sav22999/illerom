SavDocuments is a free, open word processor where the page comes first.

• Real pages: rulers, margins, header and footer, footnotes at the bottom of their page
• 43 templates: letters, reports, CVs, invoices, recipes, invitations and more
• More than text: tables, charts, diagrams, shapes, formulas, QR codes and signatures
• Write together: share with people, comments, personal notes and versions
• Files: open and save Word (.docx) and SavDocuments files; save as PDF, EPUB, web page, Markdown or text
• Works offline: your recent documents stay on your phone, and the changes are saved when the connection is back

Free, open source, no ads, no trackers.
Developed by Saverio Morelli, published under Illerom.
