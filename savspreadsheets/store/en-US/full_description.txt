SavSpreadsheets is a free, open source spreadsheet, part of SavOffice: budgets, lists, invoices, registers and plans that compute as you type.

• Workbooks with many sheets, frozen rows and columns, merged cells, filters, sorting and series fill
• More than 250 functions (SUM, VLOOKUP, XLOOKUP, IFS, FILTER, UNIQUE…), always in the standard English of spreadsheets, with hints as you type
• Number, currency, percentage and date formats; values shown the way your language writes them
• Conditional formatting, data validation with lists and checkboxes, charts drawn from your cells
• Opens and saves Excel (.xlsx and .xls), OpenDocument (.ods), CSV and its own .savspr files; print and PDF
• 17 ready templates: budget, expenses, invoice, timesheet, project tracker, grade book, loan calculator…
• Work together live: share with a link or by email, see who is on which cell, comments, personal notes and versions
• Works offline: the spreadsheets you open stay on the device and are saved as soon as the connection is back

No ads, no tracking, data in the European Union.
