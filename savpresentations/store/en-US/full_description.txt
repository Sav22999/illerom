SavPresentations is a free, open presentation editor that opens at once and gets out of the way.

• Themes and layouts that already look good, from a blank slide or a template
• Simple mode (a grid) or advanced mode (everything moves freely)
• Text, pictures, videos, charts, tables, shapes, icons and links
• Present anywhere: full screen, presenter view, laser pointer and pen
• Work together live, with comments, personal notes and versions
• Files: PDF, PowerPoint (.pptx), OpenDocument (.odp) and SavPresentations files
• Works offline: your recent presentations stay on your phone

Free, open source, no ads.
Developed by Saverio Morelli, published under Illerom.
